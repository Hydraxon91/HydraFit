# HydraFit 0.11.0

Log what a machine, a carry or a grip meter actually measured — and let the planner continue your
added load on bodyweight work. This release folds three planned updates: equipment stations, distance
as a measurement, and measured force with added-load progression.

## New: equipment stations

- **Give each machine in your gym its own identity.** A profile can hold several stations under one
  equipment type (for example two cable stacks), each with its own name and an optional load basis,
  minimum, maximum and increment.
- **Choosing a station when you log a set records that stack**, so history shows which machine the
  weight came from instead of one anonymous "cable machine".
- **Progression never mixes stations.** A station's own history prefills and progresses against that
  stack; an exercise without generic history gets no invented number rather than borrowing another
  machine's weight.
- **Exercise Progress can filter by station** — all work, unattributed work, or one station — and the
  filter resets cleanly when you change exercise.
- Stations travel in backups, including a station name frozen onto a set when the station no longer
  exists on the receiving device.

## New: distance as a measurement

- **Distance slots and distance sets.** Routines and plans offer Repetitions, Seconds or Distance, and
  the Logger records a distance for manual or guided work.
- **Carries can carry load.** A distance set may record its own weight; the distance itself is stored
  as exact whole metres.
- **Metres or feet, your choice.** Settings has a distance preference, and entered distances, history,
  the summary and Progress all show that unit while storage stays metres.
- **Distance is its own thing.** It never feeds rep-based estimated 1RM, external volume, modelled
  fatigue or automatic weight suggestions, and a distance set always replaces a repetition count
  rather than sitting beside one.
- Distance appears in the analysis CSV, printable plans, routine sharing and backups.

## New: measured force and devices

- **A fourth measurement: force in newtons.** Any exercise can be logged by measured force — a grip
  dynamometer reading, for example. Repetitions, seconds, distance and force are exclusive: a set is
  one of them.
- **Describe the device, optionally.** A force set can record the device's name and, if the maker
  states one, its rating. The rating is descriptive identity: HydraFit never converts it into force or
  into lifted weight.
- **Force is load-free.** A force set cannot also carry a weight, and it never becomes a load, a
  volume, an estimated 1RM, a strength record or a fatigue input.
- Force and its device appear in the Logger, history, the post-workout summary and Progress, are
  exported to the analysis CSV, and are restored from backups. Changing a set away from force clears
  its device and its recorded target, and switching a planned slot to force clears its load and its
  ordered per-set targets.

## New: added-load progression for bodyweight work

- **The planner can now suggest added kilograms for bodyweight-addable exercises** such as weighted
  pull-ups or dips, instead of leaving the slot without a number.
- **It continues what you actually recorded.** The suggestion starts from your latest qualifying
  added-load set and advances it with the same success streak and increment the app already uses for
  external weight; missed sessions give an increment back.
- **No estimates and no body weight.** Added load is never derived from reps, an estimated 1RM or your
  body mass, and a deload week pauses increments instead of scaling the added number.
- **No recorded added load means no number.** The slot stays a plain bodyweight prescription rather
  than receiving an invented load, and ordered per-set targets remain external-weight only.

## Notes

- **Your data upgrades in place.** The app moves your database forward automatically on first launch;
  nothing is cleared and no permissions are requested.
- **Backups made here use format 27 and need this version or later to restore.** Older backups open
  normally, and a backup from this version cannot be read by 0.10.x.
- **Shared routines use interchange format 7**, which older versions cannot read. Workout-CSV import
  is unchanged: no supported provider exports a force column, so imports never invent one.
- **A recorded force reading is data, not a judgement.** Very large or very small values are kept as
  entered; the app does not flag them as implausible.
