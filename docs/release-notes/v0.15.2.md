# 0.15.2

## New

- **Colour themes.** Settings now has an **Appearance** page. Choose light, dark or follow the device, and
  one of nine themes: the original Quiet Strength (still the default), Mata Fire, Mata Water, Mata Air,
  Mata Earth, Mata Stone, Mata Ice, Makuta and Gold Nuva. Themes only change colours. Your choice is kept
  on this phone and is not part of backups.
- **Progress list.** Exercises are now ordered by how often you trained them recently, with the latest
  estimated 1RM and how it changed shown on each row. The back button at the top right is replaced by a
  left arrow, since the bottom bar already gets you around.
- **Progress chart.** A fuller chart: a larger latest point, a dashed lead-in to the left edge and a soft
  fill under the line. The dashed part and the fill are for reference only, not measurements.
- **Settings home** is now a set of cards, like the Equipment hub.

## Improved

- **Workout card.** One main button swaps between Start set and Set completed, and the rest of the actions
  moved into a More menu, so the card is calmer. Tap an exercise's name anywhere on Train to see its
  details.
- **Planner.** Biceps and triceps get more dedicated sets on busier weeks (4, 6 or 8 a week depending on how
  many days you train), the same exercise no longer repeats across days when an alternative exists, and
  compound lifts are chosen for the muscles they are meant to train. Endurance uses two sets for accessories,
  and the default rest time now follows your training goal until you set your own.

## Known limitations

- The new screens and themes have been checked on an emulator and not yet widely on a phone. The themes are
  checked most on Settings, so tell us if a colour looks wrong somewhere else.
- Restoring a backup on a new phone starts on the default theme.
