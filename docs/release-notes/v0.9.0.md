# HydraFit 0.9.0

This release adds equipment profiles, a loading setup per profile, and a plate and
dumbbell calculator.

## New

- **Equipment profiles.** You can keep more than one equipment setup — for example
  home and the gym — and switch between them. Each profile has its own selected
  equipment, its own maximum weights, and its own loading setup. You can create an
  empty profile, copy the active one, or rename it. Deleting the active profile asks
  which profile to switch to first, and you always keep at least one profile.
- **Loading setup.** Each profile can record what you actually own: plate weights
  with how many of each you have, bars with their own weight and collar weight, and
  dumbbells either as a fixed list or as an adjustable range with an increment.
- **Plate and dumbbell calculator.** Enter a target weight and HydraFit shows the
  plates to load on each side and the total including the bar. When the target is not
  reachable with what you own, it shows the nearest lower and higher weights instead
  of an exact result. Dumbbell results are given per dumbbell.
- **The calculator in the Workout Logger.** While logging, you can open the same
  helper for the planned set or for a weight you type, so you can work out the plates
  without leaving the screen. It only calculates: it never changes what you log or
  the prescription.
- **Setup names the profile.** The setup step for equipment now shows which profile
  your choices are saved to.

## Improved

- Equipment availability and maximum weights now follow the active profile. If you
  switch profiles while looking at an accepted plan, the plan you are viewing stays
  on screen and you choose when to build a new one.

## Upgrade notes

- Your existing equipment and weight limits are moved into one profile called
  "My equipment" the first time you open this version. Nothing is deleted, and past
  workouts and accepted plans are not changed.

## Known limitations

- Loading setup amounts are stored in the unit the profile was set up in (kg or lb).
  Changing your display unit does not convert or reinterpret stored equipment
  amounts.
- When a prescribed weight in kilograms cannot be shown exactly in a pound loading
  setup, the helper labels the converted amount as approximate.
- Profiles describe your current equipment settings only. Past workouts and accepted
  plans are not re-labelled with the profile that was active when they were made.
- The 0.9.0 device checks covered reading and switching the new Equipment sections
  and opening and closing the Logger helper. Profile create, copy, rename and
  delete, editing the loading setup, and the calculator dialogs were verified by
  tests, not on a device.
