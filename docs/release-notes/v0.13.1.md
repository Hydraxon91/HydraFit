# 0.13.1

## Improved

- Wider windows use a navigation rail while compact windows keep Train, Progress
  and Library along the bottom.
- On sufficiently wide and tall windows, Train can show the workout overview
  beside training controls, and Progress can keep exercise selection beside its
  charts, records and history.
- Routine lists and editors keep a readable width on tablets. Editor actions
  remain reachable with the keyboard open.

## Known limitations

- Timed sets survive rotation without restarting the countdown. They remain
  foreground-only and cancel when you leave/background the app.
- No Samsung-specific cover-screen or dedicated Flex-mode interface is included.
