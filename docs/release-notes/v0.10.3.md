# HydraFit 0.10.3

Supersets are here: pair two exercises, follow the alternation while you train, and let the app
handle the transition timers.

## What you can do

- **Pair two adjacent exercises in a routine.** Set how long to transition between them and how
  long to rest after a completed round. Remove the pair at any time; the exercises and their
  prescriptions stay exactly as they were.
- **Follow the pair in a guided workout.** The Logger shows which exercise is next, alternates by
  the sets you have actually recorded, and uses the transition rest between the two exercises and
  the pair rest after a round. Unequal set counts finish the shorter exercise first.
- **Skip, resume or unpair while training.** Skipping a turn records nothing and comes back after
  the next set. Unpairing affects only the current workout and leaves recorded sets untouched.
- **Ask the planner to pair arm work.** This is off by default. When enabled, it groups only
  already-selected, adjacent biceps and triceps isolation exercises; it never reorders the workout
  or changes a prescription.

Supersets carry through routines, accepted plans, active blocks, backups and prescription exports.
Older data and older exported files stay unpaired instead of inheriting a guessed group.

## What does not change

Pairing does not change fatigue scores, progression rules, exercise eligibility or prescribed
loads. Custom exercises and close-grip bench press are not auto-paired.

## Data and compatibility

The database schema moves to version 60 (additive migration `59.sqm`) and the backup format to 24.
Restoring an older backup remains supported; it simply contains no pairs.

## Folded in

This release also closes the planned 0.10.4 effort/readiness investigation. No numeric effort
estimate or readiness adjustment ships: the available evidence does not support inferring actual
reps-in-reserve from prescribed-versus-actual reps, or mapping self-reported readiness onto
HydraFit's fatigue scores. Nothing was added to your data model for either.
