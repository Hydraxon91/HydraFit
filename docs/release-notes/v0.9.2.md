# HydraFit 0.9.2

This release replaces the Body map's placeholder diagram with anatomical male and
female figures.

## New

- **The Body map is now real anatomy.** The Fatigue screen's Body map shows an
  anatomical figure instead of the earlier block diagram. Choose **Male** or
  **Female** independently of your profile, and switch between **Front** and
  **Back**. Both figures ship with the app, so the map works offline.
- **A single fatigue scale.** Muscles run from a neutral tone at no fatigue to red
  at high fatigue, with soft transitions where neighbouring muscles meet. Nothing
  animates, and the scale is monochrome.
- **The artwork is credited.** Settings → Acknowledgments now names the two
  open-source projects the figures are adapted from, each with a link to its
  source: MuscleMap by Melih Colpan and react-native-body-highlighter by ELABBASSI
  Hicham. Both are MIT-licensed, and their notices ship with the artwork.

## Improved

- **Switching figures no longer moves the page.** The map keeps a fixed height and
  holds the figure you are viewing until the new one is ready, so the legend and
  the muscle values below it stay put.
- **Every muscle still has its own value.** All 21 muscle values remain listed below
  the map in both views, each as a separate item that a screen reader can reach.

## Upgrade notes

- Nothing is required of you. Your plans, routines, history and settings are
  untouched, and this release changes no stored data.
- A backup exported from this version restores in this version as before.

## Known limitations

- The figures are schematic. Muscle boundaries are approximate, the shoulder,
  upper-back and hip-abductor divisions are simplified, and the colours show
  estimated fatigue from your logged sets — not measured anatomy or physiology.
- The new map was checked on a debug emulator build with ordinary training data.
  Dark theme, larger text sizes, other languages, screen-reader traversal and a
  deliberate spread of low, mid and high muscle values were not re-checked on a
  device; that visual and accessibility polish is planned. The separated shoulder,
  upper-back and hip regions were likewise not checked on their own with non-zero
  values.
- iOS was verified by tests rather than on a device.
