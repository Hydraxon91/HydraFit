# 0.14.1

## New

- Body weight now shows a **Pull strength vs body weight** section when you have logged your
  body weight and at least two chin-up or pull-up sets and two pulldown sets in the last 6 weeks.
  It compares your estimated one-rep level on each, counting your body weight for chin-ups, and
  shows your pulldown level as a percentage of your chin-up level. It is for information only and
  does not change your plans.

## Known limitations

- The section stays hidden until there is enough recent data on both sides, including a body weight
  logged in the last 6 weeks. It never guesses or uses an average.
- It has not been checked on a device in this release.
- Warm-ups and drop-set or rest-pause cluster sets are ignored. Sets logged on a specific
  station are included, even though stations can have different load bases. At most the newest 500
  sets per exercise are read.
- Exercises are grouped by movement: vertical pulls with body weight count as chin-ups and
  pull-ups, and vertical pulls with an external load count as pulldowns. Rows are not included.
