# 0.15.6

## New

- **Session length.** When you generate a week, choose Standard or Longer. Standard keeps a day to about
  an hour, at most 6 exercises (8 with must-haves). Longer allows up to 9 (11 with must-haves). HydraFit
  remembers your choice, and the cloud planner follows it too.

## Improved

- **Every major muscle gets a weekly minimum.** The planner now makes sure chest, lats, upper back, quads,
  hamstrings, glutes and side delts each reach a minimum amount of work before it adds extras.
- **Better leg days.** A leg day starts with a squat or leg press, then a hip hinge. It adds a leg extension
  and a leg curl on equal footing, and a lunge only when your quads still need more work.
- **More variety for triceps.** The planner no longer picks dips on both upper days, and it rotates the
  triceps exercise between days.
- **Shrugs once a week.** A shrug is now an occasional extra, three sets once a week, instead of a fixture
  on every upper day.
- **No forearm target.** Forearms already work hard in rows, deadlifts and curls, so the planner no longer
  adds wrist exercises to chase a forearm number.
- **The cloud planner follows the same rules.** Its instructions now include the weekly minimums, the leg
  day order and the triceps variety rule.

## Known limitations

- Upper back can still land just under its weekly minimum on a Standard session.
- Glutes and hamstrings can end the week above their target, because squats, hinges and lunges all work them.
- The cloud planner treats the weekly minimums as guidance; a plan that misses one is still accepted.
- Exercise names stay in English. Translating them is planned for 0.15.7.
- If you added your own copy of a built-in piece of equipment, for example a custom Leg Extension Machine,
  the built-in exercises for it stay hidden from the planner until you remove the copy.
