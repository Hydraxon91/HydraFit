# 0.14.0

## New

- A five-day plan now trains upper body, lower body, push, pull and legs, so legs get as many
  sessions as push and pull. Four-day plans are unchanged: Upper, Lower, Upper, Lower.
- Deleting a logged set, an accepted plan, a custom exercise, an equipment tag or a baseline
  record, cancelling a training block, resetting an exercise, clearing a body-weight goal and
  removing your Groq key now ask first and say what stays.
- After you tap **Set completed now** on a guided set, you can confirm or correct the weight and
  reps before the set is logged.
- Train shows only this session's sets, with the last session folded away. **See all in workout
  history** opens the full list.

## Improved

- Planned weights for sets of about 8 to 15 reps are heavier and closer to what you actually lift.
  Low-rep weights are unchanged. Your existing accepted plans keep their weights; new plans use the
  new numbers.
- A deload week no longer adds extra exercises while it cuts sets.
- Train, the exercise history lookups and time corrections load far less of your history at a
  time, which keeps them quicker as your log grows.
- Your Groq key and your automatic-backup folder setting are no longer included in Android backup
  or phone-to-phone transfer. Neither could work on another device, so after a restore you add the
  key again and choose the backup folder again. Your workouts and the rest of your data are
  unaffected.
- The cloud planner prompt follows your split exactly and treats your recorded numbers as
  estimated one-rep maximums, not weights to copy.

## Removed

- The retired Gemini planner code is gone. Plans you saved with it still open.

## Known limitations

- The new confirmations, the completion sheet and the five-day plan have not been checked on a
  device in this release.
- The load change was fitted to a small sample of one person's logs. If a weight feels off, tell
  us which exercise and rep count.
- Sets with no recorded session do not appear on Train; find them in Workout history.
