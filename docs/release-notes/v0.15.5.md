# 0.15.5

## New

- **HydraFit in four more languages.** The whole app, including the setup and execution instructions for each
  exercise, is now available in German, Czech, Hungarian and Dutch, alongside English.
- **A language setting.** Settings now has a Language choice: follow your device language, or pick English,
  German, Czech, Hungarian or Dutch for HydraFit alone. On Android 13 and later the same choice appears in
  the system's per-app language settings.

## Improved

- **Numbers follow the language.** Weights and percentages use your language's decimal comma in German,
  Czech, Hungarian and Dutch.
- **Counts read as sentences.** Set, rep, day and exercise counts now use the correct singular and plural
  form for each language, including Czech's three plural forms.

## Known limitations

- Exercise names stay in English. Translating them is planned for 0.15.6.
- Plan explanations and the cloud planner's prompts stay in English.
- A few error messages, such as some equipment and personal-record validation failures and some schedule
  errors, still appear in English in every language.
- The word you type to confirm removing all your data stays `REMOVE` in every language.
- On iOS the language choice is not remembered after the app closes.
- The estimated set counts in the weekly plan explanation, for example "4.0 sets", keep a decimal point in
  every language.
- Force limits in the equipment editor show their numbers without your language's decimal comma.
