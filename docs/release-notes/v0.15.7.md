# 0.15.7

## New

- **Exercise names in your language.** The built-in exercises now show their German, Czech, Hungarian or
  Dutch name when the app uses that language: in Train, the guided workout, the summary, history, personal
  records, Library, routines, the Split Builder, Progress, setup and the import mapping. Search finds an
  exercise by its translated name and by its English name, and lists sort by the name you see.
- An exercise you renamed keeps your name in every language, and your own exercises are never translated.
  Exports, backups, shared files and the cloud planner keep using the English names, so files stay readable
  for everyone.
- **Equipment names in your language.** The built-in equipment, such as dumbbells, the cable machine or
  the Smith machine, now shows its translated name in Equipment, setup, the exercise editor and shared
  routines. Equipment you added yourself keeps the name you gave it.

## Improved

- **Extras are sized to what's missing.** The planner no longer adds a whole extra exercise for a small
  gap. When a muscle is short by less than two sets, it leaves it; when it adds an exercise to close a gap,
  that exercise gets only the sets needed (at least two), not a full block. This stops days like three
  shoulder raises in a row, or three chest flies next to a single curl.
- **Leg extension and leg curl match the gap.** Both stay on every lower day, but each now gets the sets
  your quads or hamstrings still need, often two. A lunge is added only when your quads are clearly short.
- **More arm work on longer sessions.** With the Longer session length and three or more training days
  (any goal except Endurance), the planner aims for 12 direct sets a week each for biceps and triceps,
  usually two curls and two triceps exercises on an upper day.
- **Fewer chest extras.** The weekly chest target is now 10 sets for the upper chest and 10 for the lower
  chest, down from 12, so the planner stops stacking chest flies to reach it.

- **Shorter Manage plan.** Each day now starts folded to one line with its focus, how many
  exercises it has and its total sets. Tap a day to see its exercises and tap it again to fold it,
  so later days are no longer a long scroll away.

## Fixed

- **Plan changes now reach the Train tab.** When a training block is running, changing an exercise or
  its reps or time in Manage plan now also updates the workouts you haven't started yet, so Train shows
  the change straight away. Workouts you have started or finished keep what you did, and an exercise you
  already replaced for one workout keeps your choice. Repeating the block also uses the changed exercise.

## Known limitations

- On a Longer upper day, the planner can fill every place before it reaches a second chest exercise.
- The cloud planner still uses fixed set counts; only the built-in planner sizes extras to the gap.
- A search that matched a translated name keeps its results if you change the language; search again to
  refresh them.
