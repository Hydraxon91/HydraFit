# 0.15.0

This release also includes everything planned as 0.14.0 and 0.14.1.

## New

- **Watch a demo.** The instructions for any exercise, in the exercise list and in the workout
  logger, now have a **Watch a demo on YouTube** button. It opens a YouTube search for the exercise
  name plus "proper form" in the YouTube app or your browser. Nothing is requested until you tap it,
  and the exercise name is the only thing sent. HydraFit still works fully offline without it.
- **Train shortcut.** Long-press the HydraFit icon and choose **Train** to open the Train tab. It
  only opens the tab; it never starts a workout.
- **This week.** Train now shows how many sessions you have done this week, your plan's days per
  week, and a quiet four-week view of sessions per week. It deliberately has no streak, target or
  warning: a week without a session is just an empty bar, and your plan is labelled when it is a
  deload week.
- **Pull strength vs body weight.** Body weight now shows a comparison when you have logged your
  body weight and at least two chin-up or pull-up sets and two pulldown sets in the last 6 weeks.
  It compares your estimated one-rep level on each, counting your body weight for chin-ups, and
  shows your pulldown level as a percentage of your chin-up level. It is for information only and
  does not change your plans.
- A five-day plan now trains upper body, lower body, push, pull and legs, so legs get as many
  sessions as push and pull. Four-day plans are unchanged: Upper, Lower, Upper, Lower.
- Deleting a logged set, an accepted plan, a custom exercise, an equipment tag or a baseline
  record, cancelling a training block, resetting an exercise, clearing a body-weight goal and
  removing your Groq key now ask first and say what stays.
- After you tap **Set completed now** on a guided set, you can confirm or correct the weight and
  reps before the set is logged.
- Train shows only this session's sets, with the last session folded away. **See all in workout
  history** opens the full list.

## Improved

- Planned weights for sets of about 8 to 15 reps are heavier and closer to what you actually lift.
  Low-rep weights are unchanged. Your existing accepted plans keep their weights; new plans use the
  new numbers.
- A deload week no longer adds extra exercises while it cuts sets.
- Train, the exercise history lookups and time corrections load far less of your history at a
  time, which keeps them quicker as your log grows.
- Your Groq key and your automatic-backup folder setting are no longer included in Android backup
  or phone-to-phone transfer. Neither could work on another device, so after a restore you add the
  key again and choose the backup folder again. Your workouts and the rest of your data are
  unaffected.
- The cloud planner prompt follows your split exactly and treats your recorded numbers as
  estimated one-rep maximums, not weights to copy.

## Removed

- The retired Gemini planner code is gone. Plans you saved with it still open.

## Known limitations

- The demo button, the shortcut, the weekly card, the pull strength comparison, the new
  confirmations, the completion sheet and the five-day plan have not been checked on a device in
  this release.
- The load change was fitted to a small sample of one person's logs. If a weight feels off, tell
  us which exercise and rep count.
- Sets with no recorded session do not appear on Train; find them in Workout history.
- Weeks run Monday to Sunday in your local time. A session counts once for the day it started, even
  if no sets were logged in it.
- "Your plan" and the deload label follow your latest accepted plan, not the calendar. The weekly
  card updates when a session or plan changes, but not at midnight while it stays on screen.
- The demo search uses the exercise's name, so a custom exercise with an unusual name may find
  nothing useful.
- The pull strength comparison stays hidden until there is enough recent data on both sides,
  including a body weight logged in the last 6 weeks. It never guesses or uses an average. Warm-ups
  and drop-set or rest-pause sets are ignored, sets logged on a specific station are included, at
  most the newest 500 sets per exercise are read, and rows are not included.
