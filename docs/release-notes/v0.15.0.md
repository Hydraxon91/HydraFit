# 0.15.0

## New

- **Watch a demo.** The instructions for any exercise, in the exercise list and in the workout
  logger, now have a **Watch a demo on YouTube** button. It opens a YouTube search for the exercise
  name plus "proper form" in the YouTube app or your browser. Nothing is requested until you tap it,
  and the exercise name is the only thing sent. HydraFit still works fully offline without it.
- **Train shortcut.** Long-press the HydraFit icon and choose **Train** to open the Train tab. It
  only opens the tab; it never starts a workout.
- **This week.** Train now shows how many sessions you have done this week, your plan's days per
  week, and a quiet four-week view of sessions per week. It deliberately has no streak, target or
  warning: a week without a session is just an empty bar, and your plan is labelled when it is a
  deload week.

## Known limitations

- The demo button, the shortcut and the weekly card have not been checked on a device in this
  release.
- Weeks run Monday to Sunday in your local time. A session counts once for the day it started, even
  if no sets were logged in it.
- "Your plan" and the deload label follow your latest accepted plan, not the calendar. The card
  updates when a session or plan changes, but not at midnight while it stays on screen.
- The demo search uses the exercise's name, so a custom exercise with an unusual name may find
  nothing useful.
