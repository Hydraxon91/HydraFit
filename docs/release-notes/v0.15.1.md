# 0.15.1

## New

- **Hand-picked demo videos.** The **Watch a demo on YouTube** button now opens a specific video for
  45 common exercises, such as the bench press, squat, deadlift, pulldown, rows, curls and core
  work. Any other exercise, including your own, still opens a YouTube search. Nothing is requested
  until you tap the button.
- Workout history now shows one date heading above each day's entries.

## Improved

- Tapping outside a set confirmation or other dialog in the workout logger no longer discards a
  measured set time or numbers you were typing.

## Known limitations

- The videos are links to YouTube, so a video that its owner removes stops working. The hip thrust,
  the dumbbell calf raises and the hanging leg raise use shared or general videos; tell us if one
  does not match its exercise.
- The demo videos and the new history headings have not been checked on a device in this release.
