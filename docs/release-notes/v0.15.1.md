# 0.15.1

## New

- **Hand-picked demo videos.** The **Watch a demo on YouTube** button now opens a specific video for
  45 common exercises, such as the bench press, squat, deadlift, pulldown, rows, curls and core
  work. Any other exercise, including your own, still opens a YouTube search. Nothing is requested
  until you tap the button.
- **Watch demo on Train.** Exercises with a hand-picked video now show a **Watch demo** button next
  to **Instructions** in the workout logger, so the video is one tap away mid-workout. In the
  Instructions dialog the demo button is now more prominent when the exercise has a video.
- **Exercise details.** On Train, tap an exercise's name to see a pop-up with the muscles it works,
  what you did the last time you logged it (the date, your sets, and your heaviest recent set), its
  instructions and the demo button. **Instructions** opens the same pop-up. It only shows
  information; closing it changes nothing you were typing.
- Workout history now shows one date heading above each day's entries.

## Improved

- Tapping outside a set confirmation or other dialog in the workout logger no longer discards a
  measured set time or numbers you were typing.

## Known limitations

- The videos are links to YouTube, so a video that its owner removes stops working. The hip thrust,
  the dumbbell calf raises and the hanging leg raise use shared or general videos; tell us if one
  does not match its exercise.
- The demo videos, the exercise details pop-up and the new history headings have been checked only
  briefly on a device in this release; tell us if something looks wrong.
